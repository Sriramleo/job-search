The live job listing confirms that confident communication in German and English, ideally C1+, is part of the desired profile. The listing also says candidates do not need to match every requirement perfectly and encourages applications, so this should be treated as a fit gap/penalty, not an automatic rejection. citeturn1search0

## BUG-007 — Job rejection feedback is not being converted into useful application intelligence

### Observed
The user reports that applied jobs are repeatedly resulting in a generic rejection email.

Example received after applying to MOTOR Ai:

"other applications match the core requirements of the role we are looking to fill even more closely."

This message does NOT identify a specific rejection reason. Therefore the system must not claim that German, AWS, Kubernetes, salary, visa status, or any other individual factor caused the rejection.

However, repeated rejection outcomes are valuable feedback for the job-search system.

### Expected behavior
When Gmail read-only synchronization detects an application rejection:

1. Match the email to the correct application using existing deterministic matching.
2. Record the application outcome as REJECTED where the evidence is sufficiently clear.
3. Preserve the original email/provenance.
4. Classify the rejection as:
   - GENERIC_COMPETITIVE_REJECTION when the employer says other candidates matched requirements more closely.
   - SPECIFIC_REQUIREMENT_REJECTION only when the email explicitly states a requirement.
   - UNKNOWN_REJECTION_REASON when the message does not provide a reason.
5. Do NOT invent the reason.
6. Feed aggregate outcomes back into analytics and future fit-model evaluation.

### Example
For the MOTOR Ai message:

Outcome: REJECTED

Reason: GENERIC_COMPETITIVE_REJECTION

Known reason:
Other applications matched the core requirements more closely.

Unknown:
The specific missing requirement is NOT known.

### Rejection analytics
Add metrics such as:
- Applications submitted
- Rejections
- Interviews
- Offers
- Rejection rate
- Rejection rate by ATS
- Rejection rate by source
- Rejection rate by fit-score band
- Rejection rate by language requirement
- Rejection rate by seniority
- Rejection rate by company/domain
- Time from application to rejection

Do not use a small sample to automatically change Fit Score weights.

Instead, surface patterns for later review.

### Future learning loop
Once enough applications exist, the system should be able to answer:
- Are high-fit jobs actually producing more interviews?
- Are jobs with explicit German requirements producing more rejections?
- Are jobs from certain sources lower quality?
- Are certain ATS/application routes associated with poor outcomes?
- Is the current Fit Score calibrated?

This should be an analytics/calibration feature, not an uncontrolled self-modifying AI system.

### Acceptance criteria
- Rejection emails are detected from read-only Gmail.
- Rejection is matched to the correct application.
- Generic rejection is recorded without invented cause.
- Original email provenance is retained.
- Dashboard/application analytics show rejection outcomes.
- Tests cover generic rejection vs explicit rejection reason.
- No automatic fit-weight changes are made from a small sample.
